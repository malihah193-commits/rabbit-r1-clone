support

most questions are answered in the KITE OS FAQ. for this demo:

  • create an account on the sign-up page. you'll get an email to confirm your address, then you can sign in.
  • your workspace runs a simulated task. it isn't connected to any AI yet.
  • password reset and checkout are simulated and do not send or charge anything.

terms

this website is a non-commercial demonstration of front-end design. nothing on it is an offer to sell. by using the demo you agree not to enter real passwords or personal data you wouldn't want stored in your own browser.

privacy policy

accounts are stored with Supabase (name, email and a securely hashed password). while you use the site, your browser keeps:

  • your sign-in session in local storage, until you sign out.
  • the contents of the demo cart in local storage.

fonts are loaded from Google Fonts; if they can't load, system fonts are used instead.

warranty & return

k1 is a fictional device, so there is nothing to return. in a real store this section would cover the warranty period, how to start a return and refund timing.
